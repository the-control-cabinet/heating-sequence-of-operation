# 02 As-is sequence (Evohome on/off)

[← Back to README](../README.md)

**2.1 Heating demand.** When any Evohome zone requires heat, its controller closes the associated BDR91. The **radiator** relay energises the boiler switched live **directly**. The **UFH** relay drives the UF zone valve, whose **end switch** energises the boiler switched live. The boiler fires to its own dial setpoint. (Surveyed 29 Oct 2026; see the [Evohome repo, A4](https://github.com/the-control-cabinet/evohome-heating-controls-ramses-integration/blob/main/docs/A4-junction-box-survey-and-wiring.md).)
**2.2 Zone valves.** The two radiator-circuit zone valves have their actuator heads **electrically disconnected and latched in manual override**. In manual override a valve is held open, **not necessarily fully open** ⟨verify for this valve model; assess the effect on flow⟩. Room temperature is controlled by the HR92 heads. The UFH zones are controlled by the HCC80R; the UF zone valve opens on UFH demand.
**2.3 DHW enable.** The Danfoss TS715Si timer output feeds terminal A of both hot-water BDR91s. The timer is set to **permanent ON**; with the timer off, neither cylinder can heat whatever Evohome demands.
**2.3a DHW demand.** Each cylinder's BDR91 closes when its CS92 reads below the DHW setpoint. Its output passes through the cylinder's **boiler-control module** (adjustable stat in series with a manual-reset overheat cut-out) to the cylinder zone valve; the valve's end switch fires the boiler. The module stat acts as an upper limit: it is set at or above the Evohome setpoint.
**2.4 Boiler cycling.** TPI cycling by Evohome (rate per controller settings).
**2.4a Pump overrun and minimum flow.** The boiler pump overruns for 2 minutes after the burner stops. With HR92s on every radiator and the UF and cylinder valves closing when satisfied, a flow path exists only through the latched radiator circuits while at least one HR92 is open. **No automatic bypass is fitted**, which the boiler manual requires for this system type ⟨remedy: ABV or one open radiator ≥ 0.6 kW, by a Gas Safe engineer⟩.
**2.5 Safety.** Boiler internal limits; cylinder high-limit cut-outs **verified in series with each cylinder valve motor**; system pressure monitored. Immersions disconnected (element controls removed); their supply is a separate RCBO.
